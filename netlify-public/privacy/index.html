Privacy Policy

Bernie Creator Studio · Effective October 25, 2026

Bernie Creator Studio is a private planning tool used only by Bernie Brice (@LLiLBiLL) to plan his own TikTok content. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what it does with data.

What we access

When Bernie connects his TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, the Studio can read, with his permission: his basic profile (display name, avatar, open ID), profile details, account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts), and his list of public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts. The Studio has read-only access. It cannot post, edit, delete, comment or send messages on TikTok.

How we use it

The data is used only to show Bernie his own results, suggest video ideas and posting times, and prepare his weekly report and media kit.

Storage and security

Data and access tokens are stored in a private automation account (n8n) and are never placed in this website's code. The Studio website is protected by a passcode. Access tokens are refreshed automatically and are deleted when the account is disconnected.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. Brand partners only ever see figures Bernie chooses to send them.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while the TikTok account is connected. Bernie can disconnect at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions, or by emailing the address below; stored TikTok data is then deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
